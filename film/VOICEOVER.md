# Voiceover script (original)

Tone: warm, confident, quick — a friend daring you, not an announcer. ~150 wpm. No claims beyond what's on screen.

| Time | Scene | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:01–0:04 | Intro pulse | "What can you do in sixty seconds?" |
| 0:05–0:09 | Arena | "One arena. Sixty seconds. Nothing but skill." |
| 0:10–0:15 | Gameplay | "Collect the shards. Chain the combo. Dodge everything else." |
| 0:17–0:22 | Same seed | "Everyone gets the same arena — so when your friend beats you… you know it's fair." (beat) "One more?" |
| 0:23–0:29 | Campus Clash | "And we're planning Campus Clash: qualifiers, brackets and finals for your school or college." |
| 0:30–0:35 | Outro | "ONE MORE. It's in development — request early access and help us test it." |

## Pronunciation
- "ONE MORE" — two clear words, equal stress, slight lift on "MORE". Never "Onemore".
- "Campus Clash" — crisp K sounds; stress on "Clash".
- "seed" — not "seat".

## Recording
- Record in a quiet, soft-furnished room; mic 15–20 cm, pop filter. 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, mono.
- Leave 0.5 s of room tone before line 1. Export one continuous file aligned to the timings above as `public/voiceover.wav`.
- If using a synthetic voice, only use a provider and voice you are licensed to use commercially, and keep the licence record with ASSET-MANIFEST.md.

## Mix & loudness
- Social/YouTube master: −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- VO sits ~6–8 dB above the bed (the film ducks the bed automatically when `withVoiceover` is true).
- SFX pickups at −12 dBFS peak; impact at −6 dBFS peak.
- The no-VO version (default render) is the music + SFX cut; burn in or upload subtitles for both.
